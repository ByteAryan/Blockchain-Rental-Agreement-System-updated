🔄 Rental agreement workflow

1. CreateLandlord publishes property, rent, deposit and duration.
2. AnchorAgreement terms are fingerprinted with a hash.
3. SignTenant accepts and deposits funds into escrow.
4. PayMonthly rent is recorded and transferred to the landlord.
5. CompleteDeposit is refunded minus an agreed deduction.
6. VerifyEvery action is preserved in the blockchain ledger.

📖 Smart contract rules

  • Only the landlord can create, cancel, end or terminate.
  • The tenant signs by paying the exact deposit. It stays locked in escrow.
  • Rent is paid straight to the landlord: exact amount, once a month.
  • Rent more than 5 days late is recorded forever.
  • At the end the tenant gets the deposit back minus an agreed deduction (never more than the deposit).
  • If rent is over 30 days overdue, the landlord may terminate and keep the deposit.
